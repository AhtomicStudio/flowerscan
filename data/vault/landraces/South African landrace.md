# South African landrace

## Curator notes
The traditional population from around Durban that the Durban Poison seed lines were selected from. High Times
describes Durban Poison as a pure sativa landrace from near Durban.

**Why 75 (founder review needed, the closest call in this set).** Rubric value 75: mostly narrow-leaf, with
documented broad-leaf influence or a sub-tropical origin. Both conditions apply.
- McPartland & Small (2020) say South Asian narrow-leaflet germplasm reached Africa early, and that THCV, a marker
  they tie to var. *indica*, is common in African landraces. They also report that Central Asian (broad-leaf)
  genetics were introduced into South Africa in the 1980s.
- Durban (about 30 degrees south) lies outside the tropics, so the origin is sub-tropical.
- Dutch Passion lists its Durban Poison line as "Durban x Unknown Indica" and calls it sativa-dominant, which is
  documented broad-leaf influence in a line from this population.

Against 75: High Times calls it a pure sativa landrace. If founders judge the original population, not today's
seed lines, 90 could be argued. I chose 75 because two rubric conditions for 75 are met and only a magazine
description supports 90.
