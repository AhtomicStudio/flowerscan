# Acapulco Gold

## Curator notes
A named Mexican landrace. High Times (2014) lists it as a native North American landrace and a sativa;
Wikipedia's list says the name comes from its place of origin and typical colour. It is the third landrace in
Skunk #1 (Sensi Seeds' account of Sam the Skunkman's work).

**No `base_sativa_pct` on purpose.** The rubric needs two fitting sources. McPartland & Small (2020) support the
regional group (narrow-leaflet germplasm spread to the Americas), but I found no breeder or seed-bank description
of a traditional Acapulco Gold line that I could use: the seed listings I saw in search results describe modern
feminised or hybridised versions, and one retailer summary mentioned a reported cross with a Nepalese indica (not
opened, not used). Without a second source the value stays empty, and descendants fall back to their own
`reported_type` for this share.
