# Colombian Gold

## Curator notes
A named traditional landrace from Colombia's Santa Marta range. It is a landrace node (not a seed-bank
stabilisation) because the Skunk #1 history treats it as one of the imported landraces Sam the Skunkman crossed.
Modern seed packs called "Colombian Gold" are seed lines; a separate `strain` node can be added if they show up
on packages.

**Why 90 (founder review needed).** Rubric value 90: narrow-leaf drug type, long flowering, tropical or equatorial
origin.
- McPartland & Small (2020): the South Asian narrow-leaflet domesticate (var. *indica*, the vernacular "Sativa",
  generally late maturing) spread early to the Americas. The paper does not discuss Colombia by name, so this is
  the regional group, not a Colombia-specific finding.
- World of Seeds describes its Colombian Gold as 100% sativa, from pure lines of the Santa Marta mountains.

Colombia is equatorial. No source I read documents broad-leaf influence in this line.
