# Gelato

## Curator notes
The breeder's own site says Gelato comes from Sunset Sherbert x Thin Mint Cookies. High Times (2023) credits
Sherbinski and his breeding partner Jigga, around 2014.

In a 2018 High Times interview (T.H. Caeczar, "4 Minutes and 20 Seconds with Sherbinski") the breeding is described
less cleanly: feminised pollen from the Cookies x Sherbert work, crossed back to Sherbert x Cookies. I kept the
breeder site's simpler statement, but founders should know the breeder's own retelling differs.

`Gelato` here is the seed line. Numbered selections (Gelato #25, #33, #41) are separate nodes, never aliases of
this one (docs/04).

`reported_type: balanced` follows a seed retailer's description (about 55% indica / 45% sativa, "leans slightly
indica"). High Times only calls it a hybrid. Weak source; worth a founder check.
