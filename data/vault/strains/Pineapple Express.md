# Pineapple Express

## Curator notes
**Lineage is disputed between versions sold under the name.**
1. **Trainwreck x Hawaiian** (used here). Sensi Seeds' strain round-up and Leafly list it this way, with Leafly
   classifying it as a sativa-dominant hybrid.
2. **Big Bud x Hawaiian Skunk.** G13 Labs sells a Pineapple Express seed line with these genetics, listed as 70%
   indica / 30% sativa. G13's page doesn't claim to be the original.
