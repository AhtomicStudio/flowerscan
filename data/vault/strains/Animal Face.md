# Animal Face

## Curator notes
High Times (2023): Animal Face and its sister pheno Blue Face both came from a Seed Junky pack of Face Off OG x
Animal Mints seeds, grown and selected by Fig Farms. Animal Face won the top indoor prize at the 2022 Emerald Cup.
`breeder` is Seed Junky because they made the cross; Fig Farms made the selection.
