# Kush Mints

## Curator notes
Seeds Here Now credits Seed Junky Genetics with releasing Kush Mints. The only parentage I could read from a
usable source is a retailer listing that says its product was made from "a Seed Junky Genetics cut of Bubba Kush
x Animal Mints" and calls it an indica-leaning hybrid. That's a weak, single source: a priority for a founder
check or a better source.

Seed listings for Seed Junky crosses use a selection called "Kush Mints #11". That is a numbered pheno and
would be its own node, not an alias of this one.
