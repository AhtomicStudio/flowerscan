# Girl Scout Cookies

## Curator notes
Bred by the Cookie Family in the Bay Area (High Times 2014 names Jigga, Flux and Mr. Sherbinski).

**Lineage is disputed.**
1. **OG Kush x F1 Durban** (used here). In High Times (2014) Jigga describes an "F1" crossed with Durban Poison and
   stabilised, then crossed with a Kush from Jacksonville, Florida that was later called OG Kush. Sensi Seeds lists
   GSC as "OG Kush x F1 Durban". The F1 Durban step is its own node (`F1 Durban`).
2. **OG Kush x Cherry Pie.** High Times' 25 Greatest list (2014) calls this the most commonly held belief, and gives
   the F1/Durban story as the alternative.

Type: Sensi Seeds calls it balanced. "GSC" is the common shelf name and also the post-dispute rename per the domain
review. Named cuts and phenos (Thin Mint, Forum cut) are separate nodes.
