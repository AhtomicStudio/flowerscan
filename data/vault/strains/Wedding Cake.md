# Wedding Cake

## Curator notes
Selected from Seed Junky Genetics' work in Los Angeles. Top-seller lists (Headset data) put it at or near the top
in many US states.

**Lineage is disputed.** Two versions:
1. **Triangle Kush x Animal Mints** (used here). Seeds Here Now's comparison table lists Wedding Cake this way, and
   Neptune Seed Bank lists Seed Junky's Wedding Cake F4 seeds with the lineage "Triangle Mints #23 F4", which ties
   Wedding Cake to the Triangle Mints (Triangle Kush x Animal Mints) line. This is the version usually attributed
   to the breeder.
2. **Cookies (original GSC) x Cherry Pie.** High Times (2019) describes Seed Junky's indica-dominant Wedding Cake
   as a cross of the original Cookies and Cherry Pie. Other seed lines sold under this name use that pairing too.

The alias "Triangle Mints #23" rests on the Neptune listing only; worth a founder check. "Pink Cookies" is left
out on purpose: it is used inconsistently across markets (docs/04 aliases rule).
