# Sour Diesel

## Curator notes
**The second parent is disputed.**
1. **Chem 91 x an unknown plant** (used here). Danko (High Times, 2016): in late 1994 an accidental cross of
   Chem 91 and an unknown hermaphrodite from a grower's room was named "Diesel" by AJ, later known as AJ Sour
   Diesel or East Coast Sour Diesel.
2. **Chemdawg x Super Skunk** (Wikipedia, citing a grow-guide website), or an accidental cross with Super Skunk,
   Skunk #1 or Northern Lights, which High Times (2018) reports as uncertain.

Both High Times pieces call the history largely legend. The unknown parent is left `unknown` rather than picking
one candidate.

Type: Wikipedia calls it a sativa-dominant hybrid, and High Times (2018) treats it as a sativa. I used the more
cautious `sativa-dominant`. Danko also lists "NYC Diesel" as an early name. It is left out of the aliases until
a source confirms that name isn't also used for a different plant (docs/04 aliases rule).
