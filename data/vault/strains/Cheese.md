# Cheese

## Curator notes
Sensi Seeds: the original Cheese is a Skunk #1 phenotype that turned up in southern England, kept going by the
Exodus Collective of Luton, and commonly called Exodus Cheese or UK Cheese. Modelled as a `pheno` of Skunk #1.

Seed lines sold under the Cheese name are different plants from the original phenotype and would be separate
nodes.
