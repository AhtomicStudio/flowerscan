# Gastro Pop

## Curator notes
All four sources agree: Apples & Bananas x Grape Gasoline (written "apple & Bananas", "Apples n Bananas" and, in one
review title, "Grape Gas"). MiCannaCast credits Compound Genetics; both Highest Critic reviews credit "Compound
Genetics x Cannarado Genetics". Compound is set as the breeder.

`reported_type` is `unknown` because the sources conflict: the Theory page (theorywellness.org) calls it
"sativa-leaning", both reviews are tagged indica dominant, and one of those reviews says in its text that the high
sits "closer to the Sativa spectrum". "Gastro Pop #10" (a numbered selection, reviewed separately) is not this node.

Effect tag: `relaxed` (Theory: "deep relaxation"; Pdx review: "physiologically relaxing").
