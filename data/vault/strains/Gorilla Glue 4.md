# Gorilla Glue #4

## Curator notes
Bred by GG Strains (Joesy Whales and Lone Watty) in Nevada. High Times describes an accidental pollination:
stressed plants in a room of Sour Dubb, Chem's Sister and Chocolate Diesel threw intersex flowers, and the
keeper's lineage is given as (Sour Dubb x Chem's Sister) x Chocolate Diesel. Sensi Seeds lists the same three
parents without the cross order.

After a trademark settlement the strain is sold as GG4 or Original Glue (High Times). #1 and #5 are different
selections and must stay separate nodes (docs/04, P5).
