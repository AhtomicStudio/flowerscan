# Haze

## Curator notes
A Californian polyhybrid that reached the Netherlands in the early 1980s. Sam the Skunkman says it was given to
him by the Haze Brothers with permission to sell the seeds.

**Origin is disputed, so `parents` is `unknown`** (docs/05: don't invent weights for folklore lineages).
- High Times (2014) lists Original Haze as Mexican x Colombian x Thai x Indian landrace sativas, made by the Haze
  Brothers of Northern California and later stabilised by Nevil.
- Sensi Seeds (2025) says most accounts credit the Haze Brothers with Asian and South/Central American landraces,
  but some doubt the brothers existed, and breeder Dwight Diotte says the real Haze came from Southeast Asia with
  Mexican and Colombian added later.

No source gives the cross order or proportions. Type: Sensi says Haze strains are typically very sativa-dominant.
Nevil's Haze, Super Silver Haze and other Haze lines are separate nodes, not aliases.
