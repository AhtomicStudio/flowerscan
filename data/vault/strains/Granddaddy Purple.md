# Granddaddy Purple

## Curator notes
High Times (2016) credits Ken Estes with combining Purple Urkel and Big Bud in 2003; Sensi Seeds lists Purple
Urkle x Big Bud and calls it an indica-dominant hybrid (GDP). High Times (2014) spells it "Grand Daddy Purple",
calls it indica-dominant, and describes it as derived from an old Mendocino purple strain. Parent of Purple Punch.
