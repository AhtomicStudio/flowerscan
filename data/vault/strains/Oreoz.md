# Oreoz

## Curator notes
Only one usable source states the parentage: a retailer listing gives Cookies and Cream x Secret Weapon,
"indica leaning hybrid", and names 3rd Coast Genetics as breeder (the same listing also names a cultivator in
its header, so it's not fully consistent).

3rd Coast Genetics' own page lists a strain called OOOZE as "Secret Weapon x Cookies and Cream". I did not find
a source saying OOOZE and Oreoz are the same plant, so OOOZE is not an alias. Weak sourcing overall; a founder
check against the breeder is recommended.
