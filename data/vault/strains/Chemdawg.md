# Chemdawg

## Curator notes
The seed line behind the Chem cuts. Danko (High Times, 2016): in 1991 the grower known as Chemdog bought "Dogbud"
at a Grateful Dead show in Indiana; a later ounce mailed to Massachusetts held 13 seeds, allegedly from the
California/Oregon border. The name combines "Chemweed" and "Dogbud". Sensi Seeds lists its lineage as unknown.

Danko describes the plants grown from those seeds as different individuals: the first female (originally called
"Chem Dog", now Chem 91), "Chem Dog A" (now Chem's Sister), and later seeds C, D (Chem D) and Chem #4. They are
separate `pheno` nodes with this note as their seed line, never aliases of it.

"Chem Dog" is Danko's spelling of this line's name. ("Chemdog" is the grower's nickname; not added as an alias
without a source using it for the plant.) Because the original 91 plant was also first called "Chem Dog", a
package saying only "Chem Dog" may mean that cut; the matcher should offer the cuts in "Which one?".

Type: High Times (2014) calls Chem Dog "indica-heavy".
