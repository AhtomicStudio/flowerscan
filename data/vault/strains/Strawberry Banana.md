# Strawberry Banana

## Curator notes
DNA Genetics' own page and Leafly say it comes from Crockett's Banana Kush crossed with Strawberry Bubble Gum, and call it a
70:30 indica-dominant hybrid. The Theory page (theorywellness.org) gives "Banana Kush x Bubble Gum", credited to
"DNA Genetics x Serious Seeds", and calls it "an indica strain". The second parent links to the `Bubble Gum` node:
DNA names a strawberry selection of it, which isn't modelled as its own node. DNA's seed listing on the same page
("Original Strawberry Banana x Strawberry Banana") describes its S1 seed product, not the original cross.

`reported_type` follows the breeder and Leafly (indica-dominant) over Theory's "indica".

Effect tag: `creative` (Theory: "stimulating creativity"; Leafly: "creative"). DNA Genetics' page is not used for
effects because its copy mixes in condition claims.
