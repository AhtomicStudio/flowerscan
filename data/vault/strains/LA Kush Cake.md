# LA Kush Cake

## Curator notes
All three sources give Wedding Cake and Kush Mints, credited to Seed Junky Genetics. The 2020 review and Seeds Here
Now put Wedding Cake first; the 2022 review writes "Kush Mints x Wedding Cake". Seeds Here Now also names Liberty
Health Sciences alongside Seed Junky and warns that two packs sold as "La Kush Cake" can come from different parents.

Both reviews tag it indica dominant (the 2020 one also writes "[Indica]"), so `reported_type: indica-dominant`.

Kept separate from **Kush Cake** (same parents and breeder in its sources) because no source says they're the same
plant. Merge only on a breeder source.
