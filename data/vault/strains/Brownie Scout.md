# Brownie Scout

## Curator notes
A Green Thumb Industries (GTI) strain, sold under its Rythm brand.

**Lineage is disputed.** Two versions:
1. **Kosher Kush x Platinum Girl Scout Cookies** (used here). High Times (2019) calls it "a GTI exclusive strain"
   bred at GTI's Rock Island, Illinois facility, "a cross between Koser Kush and Platinum Girl Scout Cookies".
   "Koser" is read as a misspelling of Kosher Kush, a strain High Times lists under that name elsewhere; that
   reading is mine and should be checked.
2. **Platinum Girl Scout Cookies x Stardawg Guava.** The Theory dispensary page (theorywellness.org) gives this, and
   says the strain comes from GTI.

Both versions share the Platinum Girl Scout Cookies parent. The High Times version is used because it reports the
company's own account. `reported_type: indica-dominant` is Theory's wording; Rythm's Brownie Scout also won first
place in the indica flower category at the 2022 Illinois People's Choice cup (High Times), which fits but isn't a
type statement.
