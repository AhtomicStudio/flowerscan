# Gelato #33

## Curator notes
A numbered selection from the Gelato seed line: in a 2018 High Times interview Sherbinski lists #25, #33, #41,
#45, #47 and #49 as the numbers seen out of the Gelato cross. It is a parent of Runtz, Ice Cream Cake and Lemon
Cherry Gelato in the sources cited on those notes.

Modelled as a `pheno` with the seed-line node as its only parent (P7, the fallback form). P7 prefers listing the
seed line's parents (Sunset Sherbet x Thin Mint GSC) directly; I didn't, because Sherbinski's own description of
the Gelato cross in that interview (feminised pollen and a cross back to Sherbert x Cookies) doesn't match the
simple two-parent cross cleanly. Founders can switch it once the Gelato cross is settled.

The "Larry Bird" nickname is reported in sources I chose not to use (GreenState terms); not added as an alias
until an allowed source confirms it.
