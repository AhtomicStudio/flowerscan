# Kush Cake

## Curator notes
Two reviews by different reviewers agree: a Seed Junky Genetics cross of Kush Mints and Wedding Cake, tagged indica
dominant. One writes "Kush Mints x Wedding Cake" (the order used here), the other "Wedding Cake x Kush Mints".
`reported_type` rests on the reviews' tags (weak). A Neptune Seed Bank listing for this cross returned 404.

**LA Kush Cake** has the same parents and breeder in its sources, but no source says the two names are the same
plant, so it is a separate node (docs/04 aliases rule). A founder may want to merge them if a breeder source
confirms it.
