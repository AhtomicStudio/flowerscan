# Durban Poison

## Curator notes
A place-named seed line, so it is a `strain` with the landrace as its only parent (rubric, P10). `derivation:
pheno` because it is a selection from that population, not a cross or a clone.

**Lineage is disputed between seed lines sold under the name.**
1. **Selection from the South African landrace** (used here). High Times calls it a pure sativa landrace from near
   Durban, notes that many breeders have reselected it, and lists it as pure sativa (2014 and the strain profile).
   Sam the Skunkman (Sensi Seeds blog) says Ed Rosenthal first collected the seeds in Amsterdam.
2. **Durban x an unknown indica.** Dutch Passion lists its line as "Durban X Unknown Indica" and calls it
   sativa-dominant, while also calling it a landrace.

Dutch Passion and others link Durban Poison to the GSC family (see `F1 Durban` and `Girl Scout Cookies`).
