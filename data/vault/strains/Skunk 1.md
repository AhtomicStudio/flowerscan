# Skunk #1

## Curator notes
Bred by Sam the Skunkman, who supplied the seeds Nevil later released through Sensi Seeds (Sensi at 40). The Sensi blog
describes two steps: a robust Afghan indica crossed with Colombian Gold, and that hybrid outcrossed to Acapulco Gold
to shorten flowering and raise yield. High Times (2014) and Sensi's round-up name the same three landraces (High
Times says Afghani, Mexican and Colombian).

Modelled as (Afghan landrace x Colombian Gold) x Acapulco Gold. "Afghan indica" maps to `Afghan landrace`.

Type: Sensi's seed page says 35% sativa / 65% indica. `breeder` stays empty: no note for Sam the Skunkman /
Sacred Seeds yet, and Sensi Seeds is the releaser, not the breeder.
