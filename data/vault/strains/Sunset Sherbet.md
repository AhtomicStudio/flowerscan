# Sunset Sherbet

## Curator notes
High Times (2023): a male Pink Panties plant accidentally pollinated the Cookies phenotype Thin Mint Cookies in a
garage in San Francisco's Sunset district. Seeds Here Now gives GSC x Pink Panties, bred by Mr. Sherbinski, and
calls it indica-dominant. In his 2018 High Times interview Sherbinski says a Pink Panties male seedling in a room
of Cookies pollinated them, and those seeds became the Sherbert. All three agree; the 2023 article is the most
specific about which Cookies plant was the mother.

The breeder's own site spells it "Sunset Sherbert", hence the alias. A separate "Sherbet" line by another breeder
may exist (domain review §2.4 #27), so the bare word "Sherbert" is not an alias.
