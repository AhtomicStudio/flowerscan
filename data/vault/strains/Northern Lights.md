# Northern Lights

## Curator notes
Sensi Seeds lists Northern Lights as "Thai x Afghan" and calls it an indica; its seed page says 90% indica and
that the line likely began in the US before Sensi developed its "Afghani and Thai genetics". The 40-years
article calls the #5 selection "the Afghan x Thai hybrid" and credits stabilisation to the Northern Lights Crew
in the Pacific Northwest. `breeder` is empty for that reason (the original group has no note yet).

**Expect a visible gap.** Equal weights on a Thai (90) and Afghan (10) parent give a balanced estimate, while the
breeder calls it 90% indica. The two parent populations are what the sources say, but the real proportions in the
selected line are unknown. This is the "selection bias" case in the domain review §1.4; the UI shows the
breeder's type next to the estimate. Founders may want weights here, but only with a source for them.

Northern Lights #5 (a specific selection) and #1 are separate nodes if they are added later.
