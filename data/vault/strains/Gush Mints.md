# Gush Mints

## Curator notes
Three sources agree on "Kush Mints x (F1 Durb x Gushers)", bred by Purple City Genetics, and on indica-dominant
(the Theory page, theorywellness.org, in its text; both reviews in their category tags). The bracketed part is the
`F1 Durb x Gushers` cross node. The Moon Valley review also writes "gush mintz" for the same strain, so that
spelling is an alias.

Effect tag: `focused`, named in two separate reviews by different reviewers (an "increased focus" on the
surroundings, and "a great sense of focus"). Other effect words appear in only one source each and aren't tagged.
