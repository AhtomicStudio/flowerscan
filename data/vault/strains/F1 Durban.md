# F1 Durban

## Curator notes
The Durban-based parent of Girl Scout Cookies in the breeders' account. In High Times (2014) Jigga says "the F1
was crossed with" Durban Poison and stabilised, and that strain was then crossed with Kush. What the "F1" itself
was isn't stated, so that parent is `unknown`. Sensi Seeds uses the name "F1 Durban" for this GSC parent.
Whether "F1 Durban" is still grown or sold on its own is unknown; it is a strain node because sources name it.
