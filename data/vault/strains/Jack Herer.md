# Jack Herer

## Curator notes
Bred by Sensi Seeds, named after the activist Jack Herer. Sensi's seed page calls it a 50/50 "perfectly balanced"
hybrid with three mostly sativa phenotypes and one indica-leaning one.

**Why the parents are `unknown` for now.** Sensi gives the components as "Haze x Northern Lights #5 x Shiva
Skunk" but not the order in which they were crossed. docs/05 requires a three-way cross to go through a cross node
or explicit weights, and I would have to guess which. Search results (not opened) suggest High Times writes it as
Haze x (Northern Lights #5 x Shiva Skunk); if a founder or a follow-up confirms that from a source, add a
`Northern Lights 5 x Shiva Skunk` cross node and link Haze plus that cross.
