# Layer Cake

## Curator notes
A Swamp Boys Seeds cross of Wedding Cake with a GMO x "TK Skunk" plant. The structure comes from the 2022 review,
which writes it as ((GMO x (TK x Skunk)) x Wedding Cake). The Theory page (theorywellness.org) writes "Wedding Cake
x GMO/TK Skunk", and the 2020 review lists the four names flat (Wedding Cake x GMO x Triangle Kush x Skunk #1).
Two of the three put Wedding Cake first, so it is listed first here.

"TK" is read as Triangle Kush and "Skunk" as Skunk #1 because the 2020 review spells both out. That rests on one
review; a founder may want a breeder source before verifying.

All three call it indica-leaning: the Theory text ("leans heavily on its indica roots") and both reviews' tags.
Effect tag: `relaxed` (Theory: "calm, relaxed"; the 2022 review: "body relaxation").
