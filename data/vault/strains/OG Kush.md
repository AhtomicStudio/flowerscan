# OG Kush

## Curator notes
**Origin is disputed; the sources only agree that Chem Dog genetics are involved.** Versions:
- **Chemdawg x Hindu Kush.** High Times (2016) and, as quoted by Cannabis Now, Leafly (which says it can't be sure).
- **Chemdawg x Lemon Thai**, or a Lemon Thai / Pakistani Kush hybrid crossed with a Chemdawg cutting. Dinafem's
  seed line lists "Lemon Thai x Chemdawg"; Cannabis Now reports Dinafem's story of a grower named Putz; Sensi
  Seeds lists "Chemdawg, Lemon Thai, and a Pakistani Kush".
- **An S1 bag seed of Chem 91** brought to Lake Tahoe around 1994. Danko (High Times, 2016) calls this "less easily
  substantiated" and says the provenance will always be in dispute.
- **A bag seed from a Grateful Dead show** (DNA Genetics, as quoted by Cannabis Now).

Best-supported common ground: one parent from the Chemdawg family, the other unknown. Modelled that way instead of
picking one story (docs/05, folklore lineages).

Type: Dinafem lists its seed line as 75% indica; High Times (2016) calls OG Kush an indica. I used the more
cautious `indica-dominant`. Many named OG cuts (SFV OG, Tahoe OG, Larry OG, OG #18) are separate nodes, never
aliases.
