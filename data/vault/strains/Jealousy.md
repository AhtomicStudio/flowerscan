# Jealousy

## Curator notes
**Parent and breeder are disputed.**
1. **Sherbet BX1 x Gelato #41** (used here). Seeds Here Now gives "Sherbet Bx x Gelato 41" in its strain guide and
   "Sherbet Bx1 crossed with Gelato 41" in a second article.
2. **Gelato #41 x Sherbet.** High Times (2022) reviewed "Jealousy by LitHouse" as a cross of Gelato #41 and Sherbet.

Breeder credit also conflicts: the Seeds Here Now guide credits Sherbinski, while its Permanent Marker article
credits Seed Junky Genetics with releasing Jealousy. `breeder` stays empty until a founder settles it.
`reported_type` is unknown: the guide calls it "balanced-to-indica-leaning", which spans two values.
