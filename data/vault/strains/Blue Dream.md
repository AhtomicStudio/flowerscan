# Blue Dream

## Curator notes
A California clone-only cut from the Santa Cruz area. High Times (2023) found no one who knows the original
breeder, so `breeder` stays empty.

**Which Haze is disputed.** Sources agree on Blueberry (DJ Short's line) x a Haze:
1. **Haze** (used here). High Times cites Ed Rosenthal for "Haze and DJ Short's Blueberry", and growers it quoted
   lean towards a Santa Cruz Haze. Sensi Seeds also lists Blueberry x Haze.
2. **Super Silver Haze.** Cannabis Now (2014) calls it a sativa-dominant cross of DJ Short's Blueberry and Super
   Silver Haze.

High Times also notes the Blueberry parent itself is debated (DJ Short's or another Blueberry).
