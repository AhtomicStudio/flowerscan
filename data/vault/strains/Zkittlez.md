# Zkittlez

## Curator notes
High Times (2016) gives the lineage, hedged, as a Grapefruit x Grape Ape cross with an unknown male, found by
Green R Fieldz and released through Dying Breed Seeds. High Times (2017) names the breeders as 3rd Generation
Family / Terp Hogz (Dying Breed Seeds) and calls it indica-dominant. A retailer listing gives "Grape Ape x
Grapefruit x Mystery Strain" and "indica-leaning", consistent with that.

Modelled as (Grapefruit x Grape Ape) x unknown, following High Times' structure.

Renamed "The Original Z" after a trademark settlement, per the domain review's sources. Not added as an alias
yet because I didn't open a source for the rename in this pass.
