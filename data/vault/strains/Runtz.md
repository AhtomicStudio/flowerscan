# Runtz

## Curator notes
High Times (2022) credits grower Nick Corwin with the cross, made from Zkittlez and Gelato #33; Ray Bama and
Yung LB marketed it. High Times (2019) gives the same cross as Zkittlez x Gelato without the pheno number. Both
agree, so this is not marked disputed.

`breeder` is empty because no breeder note fits yet (the sources name a grower and a brand crew, not a breeding
company). `reported_type` is unknown: the sources I read either don't classify it, or (a seed retailer's pages,
not used) contradict each other between "balanced" and "slightly indica-dominant".

Runtz is also a brand name, and the domain review notes heavy counterfeiting of Runtz packaging.
