# GMO

## Curator notes
High Times says Mamiko Seeds (Spain) made a "Chem Cookies" population by crossing Chem D with the Forum cut of
GSC. Michigan grower Skunkmasterflex (Skunkhouse Genetics) grew seeds from it in 2013, selected this plant,
named it and spread the clone. High Times (2019) describes the seed as labelled "Chem D x GSC" and uses the name
"Garlic Cookies a.k.a. GMO"; the strain profile mentions the "GMO Cookies" name from a 2017 cup win.

Both sources agree on the parents. The profile notes that the plant grows tall and flowers long for something
labelled indica-dominant; `reported_type` follows the label.
