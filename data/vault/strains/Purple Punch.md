# Purple Punch

## Curator notes
Originally bred by Supernova Gardens. Cannabis Now (2018) credits "The Village" with taking it to production;
he and Budologist formed Symbiotic Genetics, which released F2 seeds on 2017-04-20. Cannabis Now writes the
cross as Larry OG x Granddaddy Purple, High Times (2019) as Granddaddy Purple x Larry OG. The order here follows
Cannabis Now; the computed value is the same either way.
