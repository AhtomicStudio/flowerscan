# Pink Panties

## Curator notes
Father of Sunset Sherbet. Made by Sherbinski with Burmese pollen from Jigga. **The mother is disputed:**
1. **A Larry OG cut** (used here). In Sherbinski's own 2018 High Times interview, Burmese pollen from Jigga went on a
   Larry OG "Ish Cut" he had.
2. **An OG Kush female.** High Times (2023): "Sherbinski and Jigga pollinated an OG Kush female with Burmese
   Blackberry Kush pollen and created Pink Panties."

The breeder's first-hand account is preferred for the mother. The father's full name, Burmese Blackberry Kush,
comes from the 2023 article (the interview says only "Burmese"). Both versions put an OG-family mother with a
Burmese father.
