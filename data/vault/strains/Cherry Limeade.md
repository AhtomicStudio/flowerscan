# Cherry Limeade

## Curator notes
High Times (2022) mentions "Mean Gene's Cherry Limeade (Black Lime Reserve x Cherry Pie)" as a parent of Piescream.
The Highest Critic gives "Cherry Pie x Black Lime", original breeder "Mean Gene of Aficionado Seeds". Same two
parents; the order and the "Reserve" in the name differ. The High Times wording is used. "Black Lime" is not added
as an alias of Black Lime Reserve because no source says outright that they're the same plant.

`reported_type: balanced` rests only on the review's "50/50" category tag (weak). High Times gives no type.

Strains that share part of the name (Cherry Lime Runtz, Cherry Lime Haze, Cherry Lemonade) are different
cultivars in the reviews found. Search results also show other breeders' seed lines under this name; they weren't
researched and are not this node.
