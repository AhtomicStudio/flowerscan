# OG #18

## Curator notes
DNA Genetics calls "The OG #18" a distinguished OG Kush phenotype and uses it as a parent of Holy Grail Kush.
Modelled as a `pheno` of OG Kush (P7): the seed line's own parents are disputed, so the pheno takes the seed-line
node as its single parent.

No usable source classified it as indica or sativa, so `reported_type` is unknown. It appears in California's
Headset top-seller lists.
