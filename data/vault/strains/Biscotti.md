## Curator notes
High Times (2019): an indica-dominant cross of Gelato #25 and South Florida OG (a.k.a. Triangle Kush) by Cookie
Family Genetics. Seeds Here Now gives the same parents ("Gelato #25 crossed with South Florida OG"). The second
parent links to the `Triangle Kush` node, which carries "South Florida OG" as an alias on High Times' word.
