# GSC Forum Cut

## Curator notes
"The Forum cut of GSC" is the GSC plant Mamiko Seeds used for GMO's seed population (High Times GMO profile).
Modelled as a `cut` (a clone) of Girl Scout Cookies, so no generation decay (P7). Named so that packages saying
"Forum Cut" don't collapse into plain GSC (domain review §2.4 #9).
