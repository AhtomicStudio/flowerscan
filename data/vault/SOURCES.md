# Vault sources

Every source used in the vault, its terms, and how it was used. Facts only: no descriptions, reviews or images were
copied. All sources were read one page at a time on 2026-10-26 (T-007), with no bulk or automated crawling.

## How the seed strains were chosen (T-007)

Goal: the strains most commonly sold on US dispensary menus. No open, current, national ranking exists, so the
selection uses published **Headset** point-of-sale rankings (Headset tracks retail sales from participating
stores):

1. **Headset, "Are Cannabis Strains Still Important?"** (2019 data, published 2020-03-31):
   https://headset.io/industry-reports/cannabis-strain-sales-data. Named US leaders: Blue Dream, Gelato, Jack
   Herer, Wedding Cake, Purple Punch, Sour Diesel.
2. **Headset, "The top cannabis strains in the US & Canada 2021"** (2021-09-27):
   https://www.headset.io/cannabis-market-data/the-top-cannabis-strains-in-the-us-canada-2021. Wedding Cake, GG4,
   Kush Mints (US).
3. **Headset-based state top-5 and top-10 lists** published by Leaf Nation magazine: 7 states (March 2023) and
   13 states (March 2024). https://leafmagazines.com/?p=45731 and https://leafmagazines.com/?p=63021.
   See the terms note below: these were read once, for selection only, and nothing from them is in a note.

Method: count how many of the 20 US state lists plus the two national Headset reports name each strain (US markets
only; "Other" and bare "Garlic" entries ignored; "GMO Cookies"/"Garlic Cookies" counted as GMO, "Sherbert" as
Sunset Sherbet). Then research the strains in order of count, keep the ones I could source well, and add
single-list strains and well-known classics that are also ancestors.

**Dropped, and why:** Bananas (several different strains use the name; no single lineage), Brownie Scout, Mule Fuel,
Bubba Fett, Lilac Diesel, Crescendo, Glueball, Head Cheese, Blueberry Muffin (no usable source found in this pass),
Gelato Cake (the one listing found returned 404 when opened). Single-list strains not attempted: see the task log.

Result: 30 sold-list strains, plus well-known classics that are also ancestors (OG Kush, Girl Scout Cookies,
Granddaddy Purple, Chemdawg, Skunk #1, Haze, Blueberry) and the ancestors needed to trace them.
