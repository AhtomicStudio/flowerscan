# Vault sources

Every source used in the vault, its terms, and how it was used. Facts only: no descriptions, reviews or images were
copied. All sources were read one page at a time on 2026-10-26 (T-007) and 2026-10-26/27 (T-027), and 2026-10-27 (T-028), with no
bulk or automated crawling. In T-028 about 20 of the pages already read were downloaded again, one at a time with
pauses, only to check the exact effect and lineage wording. T-027's full search log (including dead ends) is in the Log of task T-027 (`T-027-landrace-decisions.md` under `tasks/`).

## How the seed strains were chosen (T-007)

Goal: the strains most commonly sold on US dispensary menus. No open, current, national ranking exists, so the
selection uses published **Headset** point-of-sale rankings (Headset tracks retail sales from participating
stores):

1. **Headset, "Are Cannabis Strains Still Important?"** (2019 data, published 2020-03-31):
   https://headset.io/industry-reports/cannabis-strain-sales-data. Named US leaders: Blue Dream, Gelato, Jack
   Herer, Wedding Cake, Purple Punch, Sour Diesel.
2. **Headset, "The top cannabis strains in the US & Canada 2021"** (2021-09-27):
   https://www.headset.io/cannabis-market-data/the-top-cannabis-strains-in-the-us-canada-2021. Wedding Cake, GG4,
   Kush Mints (US).
3. **Headset-based state top-5 and top-10 lists** published by Leaf Nation magazine: 7 states (March 2023) and
   13 states (March 2024). https://leafmagazines.com/?p=45731 and https://leafmagazines.com/?p=63021.
   See the terms note below: these were read once, for selection only, and nothing from them is in a note.

Method: count how many of the 20 US state lists plus the two national Headset reports name each strain (US markets
only; "Other" and bare "Garlic" entries ignored; "GMO Cookies"/"Garlic Cookies" counted as GMO, "Sherbert" as
Sunset Sherbet). Then research the strains in order of count, keep the ones I could source well, and add
single-list strains and well-known classics that are also ancestors.

**Dropped, and why:** Bananas (several different strains use the name; no single lineage), Brownie Scout, Mule Fuel,
Bubba Fett, Lilac Diesel, Crescendo, Glueball, Head Cheese, Blueberry Muffin (no usable source found in this pass),
Gelato Cake (the one listing found returned 404 when opened). Single-list strains not attempted: see the task log.

Result: 30 sold-list strains, plus well-known classics that are also ancestors (OG Kush, Girl Scout Cookies,
Granddaddy Purple, Chemdawg, Skunk #1, Haze, Blueberry) and the ancestors needed to trace them.

## Batch 2 (T-028, 2026-10-27)

Same selection lists: first the strains T-007 dropped for lack of a source, then the single-list strains, in the
task's order. 20 sold strains added: Gelato Cake, Blueberry Muffin, Brownie Scout, Mule Fuel, Lilac Diesel,
Crescendo, Glueball, Gush Mints, Layer Cake, Cherry Limeade, Gas Face, Kush Cake, LA Kush Cake, Orange 43, Golden
Pineapple, King Louis, Strawberry Banana, Gastro Pop, Glitter Bomb, Jenny Kush (sold total now 50). Skipped, and why,
is in the T-028 task log.

**Effect tags (T-028 rule, for founder review).** An effect slug is tagged only when at least two different pages,
by different authors, name it in their own text (the taxonomy label or a listed synonym, or the same word stem such
as "relaxation" for `relaxed`). Effect words quoted from another site (e.g. a review quoting Leafly) don't count.
Seeds Here Now blog pages aren't used for effects (they mix in medical claims). Flavors and terpenes aren't tagged (D3).

**Jeeter terms note (T-027).** Jeeter's terms are standard "personal use only" wording, not the explicit
anti-database or anti-competitor clauses that got Leafly, GreenState and Leaf Nation excluded. Jeeter is cited
because founder decision L5 names it, and only for one fact (the type it prints for its own product), with nothing
copied. Barney's Farm (below) has similar wording and was not cited, because no value needs it. A founder (or the
Q10 legal review) should decide whether "personal use only" terms exclude a source; if so, remove the Jeeter source
and Acapulco Gold's `reported_type` still rests on High Times and The Landrace Team.
