# Dying Breed Seeds

Seed company behind Zkittlez. High Times (2017) names the breeders as 3rd Generation Family / Terp Hogz (Dying
Breed Seeds); High Times (2016) credits Green R Fieldz with finding Zkittlez. Those names are kept here rather than
as aliases, because the sources don't say they are the same entity.
