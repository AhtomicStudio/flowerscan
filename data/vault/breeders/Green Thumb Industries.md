# Green Thumb Industries

Multi-state cannabis company. High Times (2019) calls Brownie Scout "a GTI exclusive strain" bred at the company's
Rock Island, Illinois facility. It sells flower under the Rythm brand (High Times 2022 lists "Rythm's Brownie
Scout"). Rythm is a brand token on packages, not an alias of the company.
