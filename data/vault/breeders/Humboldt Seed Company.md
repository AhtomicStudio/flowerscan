# Humboldt Seed Company

California seed company. Its 2026 seed guide lists Blueberry Muffin as one of its own "Signature" strains, and the
Theory dispensary page (theorywellness.org) credits it as the breeder. The website's strain pages were blocked by a
firewall when fetched, so only the PDF catalog was read.
