# Mean Gene

Breeder credited with Cherry Limeade. High Times (2022) calls it "Mean Gene's Cherry Limeade"; The Highest Critic
names "Mean Gene of Aficionado Seeds". Aficionado Seeds is not added as an alias: it is a seed company, not
another name for the person.
